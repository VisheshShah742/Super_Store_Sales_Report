# Super_Store_Sales_Report
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Super Store sales performance with insights on revenue, profit, regional trends, product categories, customer segments, and sales forecasting to support data-driven business decisions.

## Project Highlights
- Built an interactive Power BI sales dashboard  
- Analyzed sales, profit, and business KPIs  
- Created regional and category-wise performance insights  
- Added customer segment and product analysis  
- Used interactive filters and slicers for dynamic reporting  
- Developed trend analysis and sales forecasting visuals  
- Focused on data-driven business decision support

- Data Visualization  
- Business Intelligence
